## Division 02 Site Construction

### 2.1 Plank Underside Removal

- Existing construction: 6" Flexicore plank with 2" topping bearing on an existing 8" CMU foundation wall (S-1 Det 1)
- Remove existing concrete on the underside of the plank down to sound material and prepare the surface per the repair mortar manufacturer's recommendations (S-1 Det 1)
- Surface prep for SikaQuick VOH: CSP-5 profile, saturated surface dry, no standing water (SikaQuick VOH data sheet)
- Expose reinforcing steel where present and clean to bright metal using mechanical methods. (PFE p.3)
- Square repair edges to establish defined boundaries and eliminate feathering. (PFE p.3)

_(Contractor Co. allowance of FIELD LF for plank underside concrete removal, one plank, length per field survey (NAN-F-004). Any concealed deterioration, structural deficiencies, or reinforcement loss discovered during excavation will be documented and brought to the Owner's attention, with corrective work addressed through a change order if required.)_

## Division 03 Concrete

### 3.1 Plank Underside Rebuild

- Provide vertical repair mortar (Sika Quick VOH or approved equal) so that the bottom of the existing plank bears on the steel deck (S-1 Det 1)
- SikaQuick VOH: overhead lifts 1/8" to 2"; 44 lb bag yields 0.44 cu ft (SikaQuick VOH product page)
- Treat exposed rebar with a corrosion-inhibiting bonding agent to ensure proper adhesion and long-term durability. (PFE p.3)

## Division 05 Metals

### 5.1 Bracket Supports

- 6 bracket supports: 5 spaces + 1, which agrees with the 6 symbols drawn on each plan (NAN-D-004, NAN-D-005) (S-1 Fnd + Frm)
- Each bracket is L3x3x1/4 steel angle (TYP.): a vertical leg against the CMU wall, a horizontal leg under the plank and a diagonal kicker (S-1 Det 1)
- Bracket run 15'-2" between wall faces (S-1 Fnd)
- Bracket spacing 2'-8" on center (S-1 Fnd)
- First and last bracket 11" from the wall face at each end (S-1 Fnd)
- Anchor each vertical leg to the existing 8" CMU foundation wall with 1/2" dia adhesive anchors. S-1 calls for "HILTI HIT HY 70 EPOXY ANCHOR (TYP.)", which Hilti has set to phase-out (NAN-C-014); the substitute needs the engineer's approval (S-1 Det 1) [conflict; see NAN-OQ-01]
- Install all specialty bolts, including expansion and epoxy type anchors, in strict accordance with the manufacturer's printed instructions (S-1 GN)
- Bolt angle-to-angle connections with 5/8" dia ASTM A325 bolts (TYP.). SN sets A325 3/4" "unless otherwise noted on the drawings"; Det 1 notes 5/8" (S-1 Det 1 + SN)
- Install 1-1/2" x 20 ga Type "B" galvanized steel deck on the horizontal legs, under the bearing edge of the plank (S-1 Det 1)
- Prime paint all structural steel after fabrication; field connections bolted (S-1 SN)
- Structural steel other than W-shapes to ASTM A36, Grade 36 (S-1 SN)
- Design, fabrication and erection per the AISC "Specification for Structural Steel Buildings"; any welding per AWS "Structural Welding Code - Steel" with E70 series electrodes (S-1 SN)
- The steel fabricator is solely responsible for field surveying and verifying existing conditions, including the location, elevation and dimensions of existing walls and framing (S-1 SN)
- No field cutting of structural steel members for the work of other trades without prior approval of the design professional (S-1 SN)
