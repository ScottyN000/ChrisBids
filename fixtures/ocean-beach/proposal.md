## Division 02 Site Construction

### 2.1 Pressure Washing

- Pressure wash all exposed surfaces to be painted at a minimum of 3000 psi with a chemical injector and bleach solution or Simple Green solution (SW p.7 A.1)
- K.1 gives 2500-3000 psi at 4-14 gallons per minute; power tool clean per SSPC-SP3, hand tool clean per SSPC-SP2 (SW p.11 K.1)
- Wash at 3000 psi, the one pressure that meets both A.1 and K.1 (SW p.7 A.1 + p.11 K.1)
- Remove mildew with Simple Green House and Siding Wash or KrudKutter House Wash per label; rinse with clean water to remove all bleach solution; let the substrate dry completely (SW p.11 K.2)
- Clean stucco of chalk, dirt, stains and efflorescence; if chalk is still evident after cleaning, notify the owner in writing before further work (SW p.10 I.6)
- Scrape and sand peeling or checked paint; on multi-layer peeling, wash with the intention of removing all failing coating (SW p.2-3)
- Rinse all surfaces completely and allow them to dry prior to subsequent repair or coating activities. (PFE p.2)
- Inspect cleaned surfaces for underlying deterioration, delamination, or substrate failure. (PFE p.2)
- Any concealed damage discovered during or after powerwashing will be documented and brought to the Owner's attention, with corrective work addressed through a change order if required. (PFE p.2)
- Confirm all elevations are fully cleaned and properly prepared to receive base coats, finish coats, or exterior paint systems. (PFE p.2)

## Division 09 Finishes

### 9.1 Masonry, Stucco and Cementitious Surfaces

- Paint all exterior masonry/stucco surfaces (walls, ceilings, perimeter wall), decorative stucco (option to repair/resurface), and gutters, soffits, fascia and downspouts (SW p.7 A.3)
- Paint all previously painted surfaces, including utility and phone boxes (if the utility allows), ground transformers, stair systems, light poles and fixtures, pool fence, meter boxes, A/C disconnects and the underside of balconies (SW p.13 M.5)
- Prepare substrates by scraping loose or peeling coatings, spot-priming bare or repaired areas, and ensuring all surfaces are clean, dry, and suitable for new coating application. (PFE p.7)
- A full prime is required on all bare stucco and stucco repairs (SW p.5)
- Prime coat: Loxon Clear or Pigmented Acrylic Masonry Conditioner, A24 series (current SW data sheets list Loxon Conditioner as LX03, OBV-C-025) (SW p.16) [unverified; see OBV-OQ-04]
- Finish coat: SuperPaint 100% Acrylic Exterior Satin A89 (7 year; coats not stated), or Latitude 100% Acrylic Exterior Satin K62, 1 coat (10 year) (SW p.16) [see OBV-OQ-06]
- Gutters, downspouts, soffits and fascia: Loxon A24 conditioner prime, then A89 or K62 finish (SW p.18)
- Install coatings in accordance with manufacturer spread rates and drying requirements to ensure uniform coverage, consistent sheen, and long-term weather resistance. (PFE p.7)
- Hide must be complete, with additional coats regardless of the number specified; touch up suction spots after the first coat (SW p.12 L.9-L.10)
- Finish all elevations with clean transitions at architectural breaks, edges, and adjacent surfaces to achieve a cohesive and uniform appearance across the entire building envelope. (PFE p.7)
- Colors approved by the owners (darker colors fade faster and affect hide); Sherwin-Williams issues a seven or ten year product-defect warranty (SW p.14 O; P)
