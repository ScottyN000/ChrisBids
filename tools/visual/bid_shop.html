Floor log

    Replayed from the run's claim ledger (audit log and claims) and, on a live run, its calls.jsonl. Every move on the floor is one audit-log row; what the crew says is a reaction to the row they are working on, picked by that row's sequence number so a replay says the same thing every time. Three things are not in the ledger: the cat, the couriers' beer, and the closing scene. No row records a release (the pipeline never releases a bid; in the real process the estimator releases it by hand, as the sign over the door says), so the closing scene is staged: the owner walks over to the bouncer, where the finished bid waits, drops a bag of money (what is in it is the meter's total, nothing more), and the two walk out together. The bags pile up at the bouncer's feet, one per run finished on this page today, kept in this browser only. A replay run bills no tokens, so its meter stays at zero; a live call whose model has no row in the price table is counted, not priced, and the meter says so instead of showing a figure. Export a run with tools/export_run.py and load it here.
