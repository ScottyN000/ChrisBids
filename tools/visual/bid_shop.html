Floor log

    Replayed from the run's claim ledger (audit log and claims) and, on a live run, its calls.jsonl. Every move on the floor is one audit-log row; what the crew says is a reaction to the row they are working on, picked by that row's sequence number so a replay says the same thing every time. Three things are not in the ledger: the cat, the couriers' beer, and the closing scene. No row records a release (the pipeline never releases a bid; in the real process the estimator releases it by hand, as the sign over the door says), so the closing scene is staged: the owner walks over to the bouncer, drops a bag of money (what is in it is the meter's total, nothing more) then picks up the finished bid and walks it out the door, which is this show's ending by the owner's ruling (2026-10-09); in the real process the estimator releases a bid by hand, as the sign over the door says. The bid is there only when every Scope Writer run was valid, the draft is in the run folder, the audited rows include no fail and, on a whole bid, bid.txt says OK (the auditor's orphan-figure and source-hash checks are not in the log, and the caption says so); the exporter works that out from the log and the folder (a proposal.md or bid.txt older than the ledger was left by an earlier run and does not count), and any other run ends with the bag and no bid, and the caption says why. The bags pile up at the bouncer's feet, one per run finished on this page today, kept in this browser only. A replay run bills no tokens, so its meter stays at zero; a live call the exporter could not price is counted, not priced, and the meter gives the reason instead of a figure.
